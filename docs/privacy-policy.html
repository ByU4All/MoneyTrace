MoneyTrace

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

MoneyTrace does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

What data MoneyTrace uses

MoneyTrace stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Expense, income, and loan records you enter
  • Account balances and categories you create
  • Budget settings and preferences
  • Photos you optionally attach to transactions

This data exists only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
  • We do not show ads
  • We do not require an account or login
  • We do not connect to the internet
  • We do not share data with any third parties
  • We do not track usage in any form

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Camera — to photograph receipts and attach them to transactions. Photos are stored locally only.
  • Notifications — to remind you of upcoming recurring expenses. All notifications are generated on-device.
  • Storage — to read and write your database and photo attachments on older Android versions (≤ 9).

Data deletion

All app data is deleted when you uninstall MoneyTrace. You can also clear all data at any time from within the app: Settings → Data → Clear All Data.

Children and students

MoneyTrace is designed for all ages, including students and classroom use. The app is a financial literacy tool — it teaches budgeting, expense tracking, and money management habits.

Because MoneyTrace does not collect, transmit, or store any data outside your device, it is safe for use by children of any age. There is no account creation, no registration, no personal information entered into the app, and no way for us to identify who is using it.

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or from anyone else.

Parents and teachers: all data entered into the app stays on the device it was entered on. Uninstalling the app removes all data completely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
