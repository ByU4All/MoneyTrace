A personal finance tracker for all ages. Build good money habits — track expenses, manage budgets, stay on top of loans. No accounts. No cloud. No ads.

Fully offline

Budget tracking
Set monthly budgets, track spending by category, carry forward surplus.
Loans & EMIs
Track home loans, car loans, and personal loans with amortization schedules.
Friend settlements
Record who owes whom and settle up without a third-party service.
Recurring expenses
Set up subscriptions and bills so nothing slips through the cracks.
Receipt photos
Attach photos to any transaction. Stored locally, never uploaded.
